Study
8 VR novices on a Meta Quest 1, two rounds of about 20 min each
Overview

AimXR is a VR shooter where nothing is a button press. You grab gear off your body, reload by hand and judge your own throws. We watched people new to VR play it, twice, to find where the body and the game stopped agreeing.

The page plays like a match: two rounds of research, then a patch.

When people new to VR use their whole body as the controller, where does the game lose them?

Round 1: Watch

One camera on the body, one in the headset.

What did eight novices actually do with their hands, feet and eyes?

AimXR doesn’t ask you to press reload. It asks you to reload.

After a week of trying headset apps, we picked the one that asked most of a new player’s body: four physical rules, learned under fire.

Experts learn around bad controls. Novices collide with them.

So we only recruited people with no VR habits, and played two rounds each to see whether practice helped.

The instruction was on screen. Players still didn’t see it.

Players kept firing an empty gun while a PULL prompt sat right beside it. It was the first of six patterns we coded in the footage.

Visible isn’t the same as noticed. A small label competes with enemies, your hands and your balance, all at once.

Patch 1.1: Design

If the input is physical, the feedback has to be too.

Proposed, not shipped in the live game

Four fixes, each traced to something a player said or did

FIX 01Fixes boss 01

Give the hand more room

Belt slots move 20% further apart. While you reach without looking, your main weapon’s grab zone grows slightly to forgive a near miss.

Raised by 3 of 8, including P8. Gaze assist needs a prototype.
Annotated still
FIX 02Fixes boss 02

Same kind of object, same action

Every throwable becomes one-handed and arms the same way, so a player learns it once. The Molotov comes pre-lit, which was P8’s own suggestion.

Raised by P8 and P1. “moltov will lit up automatically” (P8)
Every throwable: grab with one hand, arm, release
{{ u.name }} {{ u.arm }}
Cyan = changed from today’s game. Reloads get the same treatment: bigger snap zones and a short vibration when the magazine clicks in.
FIX 03Fixes boss 03

Don’t predict the throw. Show what it did.

A dashed arc traces the throw right after release. P7 asked for an aim line before the throw, but that would do the aiming for them. After the throw, the skill stays theirs.

Raised by P7, P1, P2 and P8. Trade-off: the first throw is still a guess.
Annotated concept
FIX 04Fixes boss 04

A vignette that follows every movement

Today the screen edges darken only when you move forward. The fix darkens them for turning and strafing too, more as the camera speeds up. Drag the slider to compare.
