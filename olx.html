OLX, but it remembers

A think-aloud study of OLX’s buyer and seller flows, and a redesign that keeps the deal on screen.

Two redesign frames: a seller’s listings, then one listing’s buyers with their last offers.Parthiv’s Figma frames

Role
Led the usability tests, user data collection and the redesign; analysis and ideas shared across the team
Team
Shreya Upreti, Dipesh Ganchhaki, Aditi Khanna and Parthiv
Timeline
Three weeks, September 2026
Methods
Think-aloud usability test with 5 OLX users, task-flow mapping by memory type, a trust-metric test
Overview

At a bazaar stall, the counter does the remembering: both phones sit side by side, and the seller knows your last offer. OLX moved the bazaar onto a phone, and the counter moved into the user’s head.

The case study follows a deal: browsing, haggling, then putting things back on the counter.

Five experienced OLX users, thinking out loud

Each worked through scenario tasks while narrating what they looked at, expected and struggled with. All knew OLX well, so a slip couldn’t be put down to unfamiliarity.

The lens: every step of every flow was tagged with the kind of memory it leans on. These four did the most work.

One swipe later, the condition was gone

After a second listing we asked: without going back, what do you remember about the first? Which was cheaper, which seller more trustworthy, which phone in better shape?

The most trusted badge says the least

We put four seller profiles side by side and asked whom to contact first, and why. Phone verification scored highest, though all it confirms is a phone number.

Counter: Design

Put the deal back on the counter.

About as many screens as before. The difference is what each one asks people to carry forward.

Five fixes, each lifting one weight

Every fix traces back to something users were observed carrying. None adds a new feature to learn; each moves a detail from memory onto the screen.

From a listing to its latest offer in two taps

Sellers start from what they’re selling, not from a mixed inbox. Switch between OLX today and the redesign, then tap a pin.

By our scoring, the chat fix lifts almost a third of the load, while buying barely moves

Each step was scored for memory load and averaged across participants, then scored again on the redesigned flow. The weights show where it got lighter, and where it didn’t.

Comparing got easier, but the two heaviest steps in buying, asking the seller questions and making an offer, weren’t touched. That’s where the next round goes.

A month later, OLX filed selling chats by product

In October 2026 the OLX app stopped mixing every buyer into one stream and grouped selling chats under each listing, the idea behind Fix 03. We had no link to OLX.
